About Izdöö

Izdöö (Iradi izdöö, “search”) is Attelkom's search service for the national segment of the internet: the websites of state bodies, newspapers, companies, schools and members' pages registered in the Irad Special Economic Zone.

History

Izdöö was opened in December 2023, during the international gateway works, when foreign search engines could not reach Iradi websites. For many subscribers it was the only way to find the site of a ministry or a bank in those months. Attelkom has kept the service since the international gateway was modernised in 2026, and it remains the quickest way to find an Iradi resource: foreign search engines still do not know Irad as well as we do.

What is indexed

The index is updated weekly. It covers websites hosted by Attelkom and by the Irad Web Mirror that are registered in the ISEZ. Pages of resources on the restricted list of the Department of Public Information are not shown; when a search finds such pages, Izdöö says how many were removed. For more on restricted resources see attelkom.net.

Some subjects are covered from official sources only, in accordance with the 2008 security directives (section 4, public order).

World search

To search foreign resources choose World under the search box. World requests go through the Attelkom international gateway and are logged in accordance with the 2008 security directives. Attelkom does not check the content of foreign resources and is not responsible for it.

Adding a site

Sites registered in the ISEZ are added automatically once they appear in the national segment. Questions about the index: info@attelkom.net, hotline 105 (free from Attelkom numbers).
